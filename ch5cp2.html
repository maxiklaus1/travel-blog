Notes from Kyoto

I arrived in Kyoto early in the morning, and it was beautiful. The sun was rising, I had my coffee to-go, and it was so peaceful.

Walking through the bamboo grove, I could hear the birds and was able to enjoy the calm atmosphere before the afternoon wave of people came in.

Kyoto felt especially beautiful in the early morning, and it was the perfect way to begin exploring the city.
